Money in hundreds of Polymarket accounts can't be withdrawn or traded

On October 24 I deposited $1,000 into my Polymarket account. I can't withdraw it or trade with it, and only Polymarket can fix that. Support replied once, on October 25, to say my case had been passed on. I've heard nothing since, by email, on X or in the live chat.

What happened

On June 29 Polymarket switched to a new type of deposit wallet. Some older accounts were never moved over. They still show their old wallet address, but that wallet was never created. Deposits land at that address and just sit there, until Polymarket creates the wallet by hand.

Is your money stuck too?

Paste the wallet address from your Polymarket profile. This only reads public data, so there's nothing to connect or sign.

If it is, write to support with your account email, this address and the amount, and ask them to pass it to engineering. You can also add your case to this public thread. Never share your private key or pay anyone to get it back, and treat strangers who message you offering help as scammers.

The fix, for Polymarket's engineers
  1. Take the list. Each row has the wallet, the balance and the Polymarket user ID, or the owner's address when they posted it.
  2. Look up each user ID's signer address.
  3. Call deployLegacy(owners, ids) on the wallet factory with ids[i] = bytes32(owners[i]), skipping any wallet that already has code.


  


  

    How I got these numbers, and how to check them
    
Stuck wallets. I read every deposit of pUSD, Polymarket's dollar token, since it launched on March 31, 2026. I kept wallets that hold pUSD, have no contract, have never sent a transaction, and that Polymarket's profile service lists as an account created between May 1 and June 29. Wallets reported on GitHub were added by hand.

    
Freed wallets. I listed every old-type wallet created after June 29 that already held money one block before. A wait runs from the first deposit, or from June 29 if the money was there earlier.

    
What it misses. The real number is higher. A wallet funded some other way, or not tied to a public profile, only shows up if its owner reported it.

    
Check it yourself. On Polygonscan a stuck wallet shows a balance and no contract. Where the owner is public, predictLegacyWalletAddress(owner) on the factory returns the stuck wallet. Scripts and raw data are in the repository.
