Money in hundreds of Polymarket accounts can't be withdrawn or traded

On June 29 Polymarket switched to a new type of deposit wallet. Some older accounts were never moved over: they still show their old wallet address, but that wallet was never created. Anything deposited there is on Polygon, yet nobody can withdraw or trade it until Polymarket creates the wallet by hand. One transaction could do it for everyone.

    How to free everyone at once

    Steps and a ready-made call
    1. Take the list. Each row has the wallet, the balance and the Polymarket user ID, or the owner's address when they posted it.
    2. Look up each user ID's signer address.
    3. Call deployLegacy(owners, ids) on the factory with ids[i] = bytes32(owners[i]).
    4. Skip any wallet that already has code, since the list is a snapshot. Afterwards, "Check every wallet live" above should turn every row green.

    
  
    

  
    
    Why this happens
    
    Polymarket accounts trade through a deposit wallet, a small contract made by Polymarket's wallet factory (0x0000…3Cc07). The address is known in advance, so the app can take deposits before the contract exists.
    
    
    
    
    The factory has a function to create old wallets, deployLegacy, and only Polymarket can call it. Once it runs, the owner can withdraw as normal. Every release on this page is one of those calls.

    How I got these numbers, and what they miss
    
    Stuck wallets. I read every pUSD deposit since pUSD launched on March 31, 2026. I kept wallets that hold pUSD, have no contract and have never sent a transaction, and that Polymarket's profile service lists as an account created between the factory's first wallet (May 1) and June 29. Each was re-checked at one block, with the transaction that first funded it. Wallets reported on GitHub were added by hand.
    
    
    Freed wallets. I listed every old-type wallet the factory created after June 29 and kept those that already held pUSD, USDC or USDC.e one block before. A wait runs from the first deposit, or from June 29 if the money was there earlier, to the release.
    
    
    What it misses. The stuck list is a floor. A wallet funded some other way, or not tied to a public profile, only shows up if its owner reported it. Accounts older than the factory are left out because they may use another kind of wallet.
